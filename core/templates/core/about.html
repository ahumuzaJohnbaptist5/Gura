About GURA

Connecting Uganda with trusted repair services.

Our Mission

GURA was created to solve a simple problem: finding reliable, affordable, and trustworthy repair services is difficult. We're building a platform that connects customers with verified technicians and shops, ensuring quality service every time.

What We Do

  • Verify all technicians and shops through KYC
  • Provide a secure platform for booking and payments
  • Enable customers to leave reviews and ratings
  • Help providers grow their business online
